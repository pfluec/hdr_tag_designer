KNOWN LIMITATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------
No off-target search or validated activity score is performed.
Reference sequence only; no strain/cell-line variants are considered.
Live Ensembl mouse and human smoke tests passed on 2026-07-21; deterministic stand-ins retain offline coverage for both species.
Coding SapI changes require an identical reconstructed CDS translation. Eligible non-coding SapI changes are automatic; coding bases, the first/last three exonic bases, and six intronic splice-flank bases are excluded from that fallback. Non-coding guide-blocking, protected splice-edge changes, and cases without a safe candidate remain blocked.
Codon-usage optimization and comprehensive cryptic-splice/regulatory-motif prediction are not yet implemented.
Custom uploads currently accept SnapGene `.dna` only, require the exact supported SapI/linker architecture, and require cassette coordinates not to cross the file origin. Payload-only FASTA and arbitrary adapter/linker definitions are not yet implemented.
The result is a computational design and requires independent sequence, transcript, plasmid, and biological validation before experimental use.
